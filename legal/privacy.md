# Bouncer Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This policy explains what information Bouncer ("Bouncer", "we", "us"), operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **What you enter in the app.** Points and miles balances, program names, trip details (airports, dates, cabin, flexibility) and similar inputs. We use them only to produce your results. Bouncer does not ask for loyalty program logins or passwords. Please don't enter them.
- **Email addresses, if you give them to us.** If we add a waitlist, newsletter or contact form later, we will collect the email address and any message you send.
- **Standard hosting logs.** Our hosting provider (Railway) automatically records technical data such as IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and timestamps. We use these logs to keep the service running, secure and debuggable.

We do not knowingly collect payment card numbers. If we add paid features, payments will be handled by our payment processor (see below).

## How we use information

- To run the app and show you deals, values and recommendations.
- To keep the service secure, fix bugs and understand overall usage.
- To reply to you, or to send updates you asked for (you can unsubscribe at any time).

## Third-party services

To work, Bouncer relies on other companies, each with its own privacy policy:

- **Flight and award data providers.** To look up fares and award space, we send search details (such as airports, dates and cabin) to flight and award-availability data APIs. We do not send your name or email with these searches.
- **Hosting.** Bouncer runs on Railway, which processes requests and keeps the hosting logs described above.
- **Payments (if used).** If we offer paid features, payments will be processed by Stripe. Stripe receives your payment details directly; we only receive limited information such as the payment status.
- **Affiliate partners.** Some links may be affiliate links. When you click one, the partner may set cookies or record that you came from Bouncer, under its own privacy policy. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

## We never sell your personal data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers above, as needed to run Bouncer, or when the law requires it.

## Retention

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as the law requires. Hosting logs are kept according to our hosting provider's standard retention.
